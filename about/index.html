About

TORQUE, by The Element

TORQUE is The Element’s range for men. It starts with the one thing most men in India deal with every day: sun on the face, neck and arms. On the bike, in an auto, on the walk to lunch.

Three products, one job each

  • De-Tan Face Wash: 3% niacinamide with vitamin C and rice water. Helps reduce the look of tan. Morning and night.
  • 3-in-1 Sunscreen: serum, moisturiser and SPF in one step. Every morning.
  • De-Tan Body Wash: niacinamide with kojic acid and vitamin C, for tan on arms and neck. Daily, in the shower.

Why so few steps

Most men won’t follow a ten-step routine. TORQUE keeps it to what fits a normal day: wash, protect, shower.
